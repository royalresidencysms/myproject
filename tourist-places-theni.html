Tourist Places in Theni

Beautiful places to visit around Theni, close to your stay at Royal Residency

Looking for places to visit around Theni? Royal Residency is a comfortable base for rooms in Theni, and these ten places make an easy plan for a family trip, a couples getaway or a break from business. Tap View on map for directions, and ask our reception about the cab service.

Vaigai Dam, Theni

Vaigai Dam supplies water to five districts, which makes it one of the most important places in the region. Stroll through the gardens beside the wide reservoir and enjoy the view.

View on map

Chinna Suruli Falls, Theni

Chinna Suruli Falls is a calm, refreshing waterfall that is safe for families, and bathing is allowed. A good stop to cool off and relax during your stay in Theni.

View on map

Megamalai, Theni

Megamalai is a chill hill station near Theni, where mist drifts over green slopes. Come here to get away from the heat and enjoy the cool air.

View on map

Thekkady

Thekkady in Kerala is an eye-catching place to visit, with a misty lake, forest-covered hills and boats waiting at the shore.

View on map

John Pennycuick Memorial, Lower Camp

The John Pennycuick Memorial at Lower Camp honours the British engineer who built the Mullai Periyar Dam. The memorial hall stands with the hills rising behind it.

View on map

Kodaikanal

Kodaikanal is called the Princess of Hills. Expect cool mist, tall rock faces and dense green forest.

View on map

Cumbum Thottipalam, Theni

At Cumbum Thottipalam you can bathe on the bridge itself, with the mountains of the Western Ghats rising on every side.

View on map

Munnar

Munnar in Kerala is a beautiful place that refreshes the soul, with tea-covered hills and a winding mountain road.

View on map

Kuravan Kurathi Statue (Ramakkalmedu)

The Kuravan Kurathi Statue at Ramakkalmedu is a towering statue that shows the sculpting skills of Indian artists.

View on map

Cumbum Grapes Garden, Theni

Cumbum Grapes Garden is a garden full of grapes below the mountains, with fresh bunches ripening on the vine.
